# Investment Banking Learning Platform

## Executive summary

Translated a structured learning vision into product requirements for an investment-banking study platform. The design measures mastery through repeated, unassisted performance rather than simple content completion and supports voice answers, flashcards, mock interviews, and progress tracking.

## Business need

The learner needed a repeatable path from chapter questions to interview readiness, while administrators needed reliable visibility into progress, weak areas, and missing content.

## Product flow

Learn question → answer by voice → evaluate assistance level and accuracy → update mastery → generate/review flashcards → answer rephrased question → complete mock interview → receive feedback

## Representative requirements

- Keep questions separated by source document.
- Use stable alphanumeric question IDs.
- Track mastery as Low, Intermediate, Strong, or Mastered.
- Require over 90% unassisted performance for mastery.
- Preserve attempt history and progress.
- Generate printable 4×6 flashcards.
- Build mock interviews from one rephrased question plus two follow-ups.
- Support Admin and Student roles.

## QA and gap analysis

Documented missing questions, incorrect mastery classifications, lost history, empty flashcard/interview states, navigation limitations, and login failures. Each issue was tied to its user impact and expected behavior.

## Deliverables

- Product requirements and role definitions
- End-to-end learning workflow
- Mastery rules and status logic
- Feature inventory and page map
- QA issue log and acceptance expectations
